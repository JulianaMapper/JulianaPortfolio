Use at your own risk. This tool is provided free and as-is, with no warranty. It changes settings on your Yoto Player through Yoto’s own API, and Yoto could change or remove that feature at any time. I’m not responsible for any problems with your player, account, or cards that come from using it.

What this does

Your Yoto Player has a right-hand button. With this tool you pick up to 4 cards for Day mode and up to 4 cards for Night mode, and the button plays them. Order matters: the first card in the list is what a double press plays.

Sign in with your own Yoto account, pick your player, choose your cards, and save. That's it.

Kids can’t skip ahead. A card started from the button plays straight through, and the dials won’t jump to the next chapter. If you want variety, turn on shuffle for that card in the Yoto app.
Expect an "unverified app" warning. When you sign in, Yoto will warn you that this app isn't verified. That's expected: Yoto doesn't verify apps that change player settings. The sign-in screen lists exactly what it asks for.
Everything stays in your browser. This page talks directly to Yoto. Nothing is sent to me, and there are no analytics or trackers. Your sign-in and your last choices are saved in this browser only. Sign out clears your sign-in, and Forget saved choices clears your lists.
It uses a feature Yoto calls beta. It worked on a 3rd-generation Yoto Player without the beta turned on. It may not work on every player.
It can't show what's currently set. Yoto doesn't let this page read your player's existing shortcut list, so you'll always start from what you last saved here (in this browser), not from what the player has now.
