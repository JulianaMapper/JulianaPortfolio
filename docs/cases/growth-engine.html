Listen

Growth is a listening problem before it’s a distribution problem.

The audience (public health practitioners, local government staff, the geospatial community) wasn’t asking to be marketed to. They were asking real questions about AI, data, and their own overloaded workflows. So the content strategy became simple: answer the actual questions, in public, consistently, and let trust compound.

Ask Why

Know, like, trust is a pipeline stage. Treat it like one.

B2G sales cycles are long and trust-gated. Advertising can’t shortcut that; a body of useful work can. The bet: if prospects arrive already trusting you (because they’ve read your posts, attended your community of practice, or seen you keynote), the first sales conversation starts in the middle instead of at the beginning. That’s exactly how it has played out.

Build

The engine, part by part

The self-taught stack: website, SEO, email, long-form writing, and short-form video, learned because no one else was going to do it
An organic mailing list: 1,000+ subscribers at zero ad spend, grown through content, events, and giveaways that people actually wanted
The AI for Public Health Community of Practice: a recurring, no-sales-pitch room where the field learns together; the most trusted channel we have
Content as a product with a feedback loop: formats tested, performance measured, strategy adjusted; the same experiment discipline as any product I’ve run
A personal brand that compounds: consistent public work turned into invited keynotes at national and state conferences, which turn into inbound trust
Impact
Prospects arrive already trusting us. That’s the whole point.

The engine runs every week: content ships, the list grows, the community meets, and the speaking invitations keep coming. Three keynotes so far, all earned. For a two-person company selling into government, that trust is the growth strategy.

And it’s transferable. The playbook is simple: answer real questions in public, build the room where your field learns, treat content like a product. It works in any domain where trust gates the sale.
